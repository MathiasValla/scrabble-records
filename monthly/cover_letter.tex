\begin{letter}{Editors\\The American Mathematical Monthly}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the manuscript ``How much can a Scrabble move score? Exact
records from one tile to seven'' for publication in \textit{The American
Mathematical Monthly}.

High-scoring Scrabble constructions have circulated for many years. While
looking into these records, I realized that I could find no previous proof that
the proposed maxima were optimal. This manuscript gives computer-assisted
proofs for moves using one through seven tiles under the NWL23 and CSW24
dictionaries, together with reproducible certificates and legal constructions.

I hope that the problem and its elementary combinatorial ideas will be of
interest to Monthly readers. The manuscript includes a disclosure of the use of
OpenAI Codex (GPT-5) for computer-code verification and text editing. Thank you
for considering the manuscript.

\closing{Sincerely,}
